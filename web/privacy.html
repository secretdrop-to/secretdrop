Privacy Policy

SecretDrop is built around one principle: we cannot share what we never had.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you use SecretDrop. Our service is designed to collect as little as technically possible. The operator of this service is reachable via the contact details at the bottom of this page.

2. What We Collect

SecretDrop itself stores no personal data, only the technical minimum needed to deliver your secrets. Specifically:

  • No accounts, names, email addresses, or registration required.
  • No access logs and no stored IP addresses. Our server keeps IP addresses only in memory, for short-lived rate limits against abuse. They are never written to disk and never linked to secrets.
  • No readable content, file names, or file counts. For each secret, the server keeps only its ID, the encrypted data, a random salt, a verification tag, its expiry time, and a counter for the 5-attempt limit (see section 5). Apart from its total size, the encrypted data reveals nothing.
  • No cookies, fingerprinting, session tracking, or analytics of any kind on our part. Your browser keeps only your language and theme choice, in session storage, which is cleared when you close the tab.
  • No advertising networks, tracking pixels, or analytics. The only third-party service is Cloudflare on the clearnet site (see section 3). The Tor Onion service loads nothing from third-party servers.

3. Cloudflare

SecretDrop uses Cloudflare as a reverse proxy and DDoS protection layer for the clearnet site. As a result, all clearnet traffic passes through Cloudflare's network before reaching our server. As long as Cloudflare delivers our code unchanged, it never sees your secret in readable form. It does see technical data such as IP addresses, browser details, request times, and the secret ID, so it can link the sender and the recipient of a secret.

This means Cloudflare, not SecretDrop, is the first point of contact for your clearnet requests, including the Turnstile check. We do not control what Cloudflare logs, and Cloudflare may set its own technical cookies. You can review Cloudflare's privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Cloudflare Turnstile (clearnet only): The clearnet site uses Cloudflare Turnstile to distinguish humans from bots. It loads a script from Cloudflare only when you start writing a secret and may analyze basic browser signals. It sets no tracking cookies. This is the only script from a third-party server that runs on the site, and only on the clearnet version. On the create page it runs next to your message and the finished link, so there you also rely on Cloudflare.

4. Client-Side Encryption

All encryption and decryption happens entirely in your browser: your password or auto-generated key never reaches our server, which only ever receives ciphertext it cannot read (plus the values listed in section 2), and without the password or URL fragment the stored data is worthless to anyone. See our FAQ for more on how the encryption works, and our Security page for the full technical details.

5. Data Retention

Encrypted payloads are stored only as ciphertext that we cannot read. They are never replicated to other machines or included in any backups. They are deleted right before our server sends them and cannot be opened again, even if the download breaks off. To protect against password-guessing attacks on leaked links, the server allows a maximum of 5 retrieval attempts with the wrong password. After the fifth failed attempt the secret is deleted. If a secret is never retrieved, it is automatically purged after the expiry window selected at creation (1 hour to 7 days for messages, 1 hour to 3 days for files). Because expiry times are rounded up to the next ten minutes, a secret can stay up to ten minutes longer. Deletion is irreversible.

Password verification works with a verification tag (HMAC-SHA256) that your browser derives from the password after Argon2id. The server only compares this tag and never learns the password. Anyone who has only the link never receives the encrypted data and gets at most 5 attempts, so they cannot guess offline. Someone holding a copy of our database, or Cloudflare on the clearnet site, could try passwords offline, but each guess costs a full Argon2id computation, which a long passphrase makes impractical. Secrets without a password are not affected, because their random key cannot be guessed and never reaches our server.

6. Legal Requests & Transparency

Should we ever receive a court order, warrant, or gag order that compromises the guarantees stated here, our Warrant Canary will no longer be renewed. Because we store no personal data and cannot decrypt stored content, any legal demand for user data would yield nothing of value.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

Since we store no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. If you believe we hold data about you or have any questions regarding this policy, you may contact us via encrypted email at any time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if our technical setup changes. The date at the bottom of this page always reflects the last revision. With any such update, we will always do our best to protect your privacy.
